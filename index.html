FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to what clients ask us most.

How much customization can you do for our brand?

Complete customization! We can wrap our cart with your logo and colors, create custom cup designs, and even print your company logo or messages directly on latte foam using our latte art printer.

How far in advance should we book?

For standard events, 2-3 weeks is ideal. For custom branding (cart wraps, special cups), we recommend 4-6 weeks to ensure proper design and production time.

Do you handle corporate billing and invoicing?

Absolutely! We provide professional invoicing, can work with your procurement department, and accept corporate payment methods. We're fully insured for corporate events.

Can you handle large events with 500+ guests?

Yes! For large events, we deploy multiple carts with coordinated service teams to efficiently serve high volumes.

Do you offer non-coffee options?

Absolutely — tea, hot chocolate, matcha, and seasonal options, plus dietary accommodations like oat milk and other dairy-free alternatives.

What happens if there's bad weather?

Our carts are mobile and can be set up indoors or under covered areas. We'll work with you on a backup plan ahead of time.

Is Good Pick a coffee shop?

No. Good Pick is a mobile coffee cart for events. We have no storefront. Our event baristas bring a fully equipped espresso and matcha bar to your office, venue, set, or home.

Where do you offer coffee cart catering?

We serve Los Angeles, Malibu, the San Fernando Valley, and Orange County, and can travel elsewhere in Southern California. Contact us to confirm your venue.

What is included in coffee cart catering?

Every booking includes a staffed coffee cart, professional baristas, an espresso and specialty drink menu, setup, and breakdown. Branding, matcha, and extra carts are available as add-ons.
