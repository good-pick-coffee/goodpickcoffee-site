FAQ

Common Questions

What makes Gesha coffee different?

Gesha is a rare coffee varietal prized for its delicate, floral, tea-like character — very different from a typical espresso roast, and priced accordingly for its quality and scarcity.

How many guests can the tasting accommodate?

Our guided format works best in small groups; for larger events we can run multiple tasting rounds or stations.

Can we pair this with a full coffee cart?

Yes — Gesha tasting is often booked as a premium add-on alongside our standard espresso or matcha cart service.

What is Gesha coffee?

Gesha, also spelled Geisha, is a coffee variety known for floral, tea-like flavors. It is usually enjoyed black.

How many guests can join a tasting?

Group size depends on the format. Tell us your headcount and we will recommend a setup.
