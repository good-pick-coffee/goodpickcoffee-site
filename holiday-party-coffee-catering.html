FAQ

Common Questions

What happens if there's bad weather?

Our cart is mobile and can be set up indoors or under covered areas. We'll work with you on a backup plan ahead of time.

How far in advance should we book for the holidays?

Holiday season books up fast — we recommend securing your date at least 4-6 weeks out, especially for December bookings.

Do you offer non-coffee options?

Yes — tea, hot chocolate, matcha, and seasonal options, plus dairy-free alternatives like oat milk.

Do you serve private homes?

Yes. We need a flat area for the cart and access to power. We will go over the details when you book.

Do you offer seasonal drinks?

Yes. We can build a festive menu with seasonal specials. Ask about current options when you request a quote.
