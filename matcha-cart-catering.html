FAQ

Common Questions

Can we have both matcha and coffee at our event?

Yes — the matcha cart can run alongside our full espresso menu, or as a standalone bar depending on your event.

What matcha drinks do you offer?

Ceremonial matcha lattes, iced strawberry matcha, and seasonal matcha specials — see our full menu for details.

Do you accommodate dietary preferences?

Yes — oat, almond, soy, and other dairy-free milk options are available at no extra charge.

Can the matcha cart run without coffee?

Yes. The matcha cart can run as a standalone bar.

Do you offer dairy-free matcha?

Yes. We offer oat milk and other dairy-free options.
