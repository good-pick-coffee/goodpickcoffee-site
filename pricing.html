FAQ

Pricing Questions

How much does a coffee cart cost in Los Angeles?

It depends on your guest count, event duration, location, and any add-ons — so we don't publish flat rates. Fill out our quick quote form and we'll send a custom package within a few hours.

What's included in every package?

Setup and breakdown, all equipment and supplies, premium coffee beans, non-coffee options, insurance coverage, and a dedicated event coordinator. Custom branding and add-ons are available separately.

How do add-ons affect the total cost?

Add-ons like custom branding, a matcha cart, or extra service hours are scoped individually and included as line items in your custom quote.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, a deposit secures your date, with the balance due before the event. Exact terms will be outlined in your quote.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms are outlined in your contract at booking. Reach out as early as possible if your plans change and we'll work with you.

Is gratuity included in the price?

Gratuity is not automatically included and is always appreciated but never required.
