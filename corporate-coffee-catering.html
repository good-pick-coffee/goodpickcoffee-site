FAQ

Common Questions

Do you handle corporate billing and invoicing?

Yes — we provide professional invoicing, can work with your procurement department, and accept corporate payment methods. We're fully insured for corporate events.

Can you handle large events with 500+ guests?

Yes. For large events, we deploy multiple carts with coordinated barista teams to efficiently serve high volumes.

How far in advance should we book?

For standard corporate events, 2-3 weeks is ideal. For custom branding, we recommend 4-6 weeks.

How much does corporate coffee catering cost in Los Angeles?

Pricing depends on service hours, headcount, and branding. See our pricing page for packages, or send your details through the quote form for a custom quote.

Do you serve offices and coworking spaces?

Yes. We set up in lobbies, courtyards, and event spaces. Share the building's load-in and insurance rules and we will plan around them.
