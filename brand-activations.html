FAQ

Common Questions

How much customization can you do for our brand?

Complete customization — we can wrap the cart with your logo and colors, create custom cup designs, add LED screens, and print your logo directly on latte foam.

How far in advance should we book for custom branding?

We recommend 4-6 weeks for cart wraps, custom cups, and screen content to ensure proper design and production time.

Can you handle large-scale activations?

Yes — for larger footprints we deploy multiple branded carts with coordinated barista teams.
